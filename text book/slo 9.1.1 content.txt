Topic: Ease of Use and Efficiency of Digital Devices

A digital device should help a user perform a task easily and efficiently.

EASE OF USE

Ease of use means how simple a digital device is to understand and operate.

A device is easy to use when:
- its buttons and menus are clear;
- the user can learn it quickly;
- tasks can be performed without confusion.

Example:
A smartphone with clear icons and simple menus is easy to use.


EFFICIENCY

Efficiency means completing a task with less time and effort.

A device is efficient when:
- it performs tasks quickly;
- it reduces unnecessary steps;
- it can automate or speed up work.

Example:
A calculator is efficient because it performs calculations much faster than doing them manually.


KEY DIFFERENCE

Ease of use = How easy is the device to operate?

Efficiency = How quickly and effectively does the device help complete a task?


REAL-LIFE EXAMPLE: ATM

Ease of use:
An ATM gives clear instructions and simple options, making it easy to operate.

Efficiency:
It allows a person to withdraw money quickly without waiting for a bank employee.


SUMMARY

A good digital device should be easy to understand and should help the user complete tasks quickly with minimum effort.
